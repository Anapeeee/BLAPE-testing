About BLAPE

We built this because we needed it ourselves.

BLAPE started as an internal outreach system for a B2B consulting business. We needed a way to generate meetings without posting daily content, running ads or paying for introductions. So we built one.

The system worked well enough that other businesses started asking us to build it for them. That is what BLAPE is now.

We are not a traditional agency. We do not sell retainers for things you do not need. We build systems, hand them over, and stay involved as long as we are useful.
