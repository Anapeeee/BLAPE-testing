Case Study

1.4% to 9% reply rate in 4 weeks.

A gym had been cold emailing lapsed members for 3 months. Opens were fine. Replies weren't. We found three problems: generic subject line, "we" instead of personal opening, and no DKIM configured. We rewrote the sequence and fixed DNS. Replies jumped in week one.
