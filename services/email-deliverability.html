The Technical Setup

What we configure and why it matters

Most people skip these steps. That is why their emails go to spam.

SPF

Sender Policy Framework

Tells receiving servers which IPs are authorised to send email on behalf of your domain. Without it, you fail basic spam checks.

DKIM

Domain Keys Identified Mail

Adds a cryptographic signature to every email you send. Proves the message has not been tampered with in transit.

DMARC

Domain-based Message Authentication

Tells receiving servers what to do if SPF or DKIM fails. Protects your domain from spoofing and phishing attacks.

WARM-UP

Mailbox Warm-Up

New mailboxes need to build sending reputation gradually. We automate this process correctly so you can scale without hitting limits.
